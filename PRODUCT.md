# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is nontechnical business owners and decision-makers who know their operations but may not know which software or AI approach is appropriate. They need to understand what Solvin can build, see credible proof, and begin a useful conversation without translating their needs into technical requirements.

Startup founders and technical evaluators are a secondary audience. They should be able to inspect deeper implementation detail without forcing technical language into the primary journey.

The business development manager is an internal user of the site: the portfolio must be clear and impressive enough to support prospect conversations and follow-up.

## Product Purpose

Solvin's website is a founder-led portfolio and business-development surface. It should help prospects quickly understand what Solvin builds, experience the quality of the work, and describe a business problem in plain language.

Success means a qualified prospect can identify a relevant capability or project, trust the person behind the work, and begin either a guided requirements conversation or a direct project inquiry.

## Positioning

Solvin combines direct founder judgment with hands-on product design and engineering across web applications, AI-native products, websites, mobile applications, and React Native desktop applications. It turns unclear business needs into understandable, working software rather than selling an abstract technology transformation.

## Operating Context

Most Solvin projects are hosted as subdomains of `solvin.co`. The main site acts as the index and narrative layer connecting those live products, demonstrations, and future client projects.

Prospects may arrive through founder referrals or business-development outreach. Many will be business owners with limited technical vocabulary and limited patience for long discovery questionnaires.

The guided assistant should ask a small number of adaptive, immediately answerable questions, help the prospect understand possible solutions, and produce a concise requirements summary for Solvin.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web application design and development.
- AI-native applications, agents, assistants, and workflow systems.
- Marketing and portfolio website design and development.
- Mobile applications and React Native desktop applications where appropriate.
- The site should support a portfolio of owned products and demonstrations now, with client work added later as it becomes publishable.
- Portfolio content must clearly distinguish owned products, demonstrations, experiments, and future client work. It must not invent clients, outcomes, testimonials, or performance claims.
- The proposed prospect assistant should use Luna as its primary model to control operating cost, subject to technical validation during implementation.
- The initial lead workflow may store conversations and structured requirements, or send a concise summary through Resend. A full CRM/dashboard is an open implementation decision and should be earned by actual usage.
- The Assistant uses deterministic information-gap planning for topic selection, scoring, consent, authorization, and persistence. Luna extracts facts and proposes wording, but cannot decide completion or perform database operations.

## Brand Commitments

The identity is founder-led Solvin: the founder is the source of trust and judgment; Solvin is the professional studio through which the work is delivered.

The brand behaves like a gentleman: composed, direct, dependable, exact, restrained, and respectful of the visitor's time. It should demonstrate those qualities through clarity and delivery rather than repeatedly describing itself as stoic or gentlemanly.

The name Solvin, existing logo family, monochrome foundation, and restrained blue accent are recognized assets. The redesign may refine their use but should preserve recognizability.

Avoid AI hype, inflated claims, faux-corporate language, decorative robots, neon technology imagery, and superficial luxury clichés such as suits, watches, whiskey, or excessive heritage styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- A functioning contextual Assistant with deterministic topic planning, structured model extraction, response-quality validation, session recovery, consent-aware lead capture, and tests.
- A scroll-controlled cinematic homepage demonstration that moves from reflected software to the recognizable Solvin mark.
- The current Solvin website, brand guide, logo assets, content model, and working contact flow.
- Owned products, demonstrations, and experiments can be presented as the initial portfolio.
- Client projects are currently being built and are not yet available as public proof.
- No testimonials, named client outcomes, or performance claims have been confirmed and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Show working systems before describing capabilities.
2. Speak in the language of business problems and observable outcomes.
3. Earn trust through honest scope, visible craft, and direct founder accountability.
4. Ask less, infer carefully, and make every prospect question easy to answer.
5. Use AI for understanding and assistance while keeping consequential rules deterministic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain keyboard accessible, responsive, readable at enlarged text sizes, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. Signature animation must have a coherent static or low-motion equivalent, and the chatbot must not require technical knowledge to use successfully.
